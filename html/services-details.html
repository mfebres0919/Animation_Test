Kitchen Remodeling

The kitchen is the room your household actually lives in, and it is the one a bad remodel punishes you for every single day. We plan the layout around how you cook and gather first, then pick finishes — never the other way around.

A full kitchen takes us four to seven weeks on site, depending on whether walls move and how long your cabinetry takes to arrive. You get one project manager, a written schedule before demolition starts, and a dust wall between the work and the rest of your house. Plumbing, electrical and tile are all handled by our own crew, so nobody is waiting on a subcontractor who booked another job.

  • Layout and lighting plan drawn before anything is ordered
  • Cabinetry, counters, backsplash and flooring as one package
  • Permits pulled and inspections scheduled by us, not by you
  • Written fixed price — change orders only if you ask for a change

  • One Crew Throughout

    The same team that tears out the old kitchen installs the new one. No handoffs between trades, no week-long gaps waiting for somebody else's schedule to clear.

  • A Date You Can Plan Around

    You get a finish date in writing before we start, and a short update every Friday telling you where the job stands against it.

How long does a full kitchen remodel take?

Four to seven weeks on site for most kitchens. Moving plumbing or taking out a load-bearing wall adds roughly a week; custom cabinetry can add longer, but that lead time runs before we ever start demolition, so it does not leave you without a kitchen.

Can I stay in the house during the work?

Almost everyone does. We seal the work area behind a dust wall, run air scrubbers, and can set up a temporary sink and counter elsewhere in the house so you are not eating out for a month.

Do you handle permits and inspections?

Yes. Permits, inspection scheduling and the final sign-off are all ours. The cost is in the quote you approve — it is not a line item that appears later.

What does a kitchen remodel cost in Houston?

Most of the kitchens we finish land between $35,000 and $90,000. The spread is cabinetry and whether the layout changes. We will give you a real number after one visit, not a range.

What happens if something goes wrong afterwards?

Our workmanship is covered for two years, and we pass through the manufacturer warranty on everything we install. Call the same number you have been calling all along — it reaches the same people.
